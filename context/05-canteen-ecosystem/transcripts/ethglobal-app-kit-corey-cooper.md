## Summary (10 bullets)

1. Corey Cooper (Circle DevRel) at an ETHGlobal event, covering App Kits and a Circle Agent Stack demo.
2. App Kits design goal: multi-chain flows from Arc to other ecosystems (EVM and non-EVM), not only Arc.
3. Bridge: an Arc-to-Solana cross-chain send in about 4 lines, versus a couple of hundred lines of raw CCTP.
4. Swap: DEX partners give access to thousands of tokens. On Arc day one there is USDC and EURC (onchain FX) and cirBTC.
5. Unified Balance: Gateway integration drops from about 300 lines to about 5. Deposit on Arc, spend on Base, Solana or Avalanche.
6. App Kits come pre-wired to Circle's RPC. Adapters exist for Circle wallets, viem and ethers (bring your own wallet).
7. Monetization parameter: pass a fee in bps plus a payout address and get paid at transaction time.
8. Live demo: deposit $5 into the Gateway balance on Arc, then pay out $5 USDC to a Base address from that balance.
9. Agent Stack demo: Claude plus Circle CLI paying a $1 x402 **nanopayment** from a Gateway balance on Arc testnet to an endpoint wrapped with **Proceeds (myproceeds.xyz)**, the same company behind TestMint. Proceeds wraps any API with x402, MPP and Circle nanopayments.
10. Takeaway for Tameion: use Proceeds to monetize your agent's own API (RFB 2 and 4 style revenue), and App Kits for treasury movement in about 5 lines.
