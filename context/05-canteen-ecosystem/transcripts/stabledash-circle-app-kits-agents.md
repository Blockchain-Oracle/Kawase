## Summary (10 bullets)

1. Anthony Colani (Circle, head of ecosystem growth) on App Kits, demoed on the open-source **arc-fintech** treasury app (the Tameion RFB 1 sample).
2. App Kits are composable SDKs that abstract the onchain stack: Bridge (CCTP), Swap (USDC, EURC, cirBTC, USDT, natives), Unified Balance (Gateway), and Send.
3. Built-in monetization: add your own fee (bps plus payout address) to any kit flow without writing or auditing fee contracts.
4. Use the whole App Kit SDK or individual kits (Bridge Kit, Swap Kit, Unified Balance Kit).
5. arc-fintech demo: a treasury dashboard over Circle Developer-Controlled Wallets across 4 chains, with wallet creation, a multi-chain balance view, and CCTP rebalancing (1,000 USDC from Arc to Base, "just that instantly").
6. Gateway demo: move funds from several wallets into the unified balance and then spend the whole ~$10k on any chain, even one with little local liquidity.
7. Pain it removes: pre-funding every chain, idle capital, constant bridging and rebalancing risk, and gas paid again and again.
8. Circle wallets are chain-agnostic (Arc plus many chains). Bring-your-own wallet works too.
9. Agents are "a new customer base". The Circle Agent Stack = CLI (provision agent wallets) + x402 + Nanopayments (sub-cent) + Agent Marketplace (agents.circle.com).
10. Direct template for RFB 1 (Intelligent Business Treasury): fork arc-fintech and add the agent decision layer on top.
