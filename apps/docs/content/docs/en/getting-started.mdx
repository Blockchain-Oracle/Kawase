## Open the app

<Tabs items={['Web', 'iPhone', 'Android']}>
  <Tab value="Web">
    Open [app.getkawase.xyz](https://app.getkawase.xyz) in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone. You can install
    it as an app from the browser ([web and desktop](./features/web-and-desktop.mdx)). On the web, an **email code**
    stands in for Face ID wherever a payment needs your confirmation.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="iPhone">
    Kawase for iPhone is a public beta on Apple's **TestFlight**. Open
    [getkawase.xyz/download](https://getkawase.xyz/download) on the iPhone: it opens the TestFlight invitation as soon
    as Apple's beta review lets testers in, and the web app until then.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Android">
    Download the APK from [getkawase.xyz/download](https://getkawase.xyz/download), open it and allow the install when
    Android asks. On Android your confirmation is your **fingerprint or screen lock**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Testnet and mainnet

The **network pill** at the top of Home says where you are and switches in one tap, both ways. Testnet is free and
behaves like the real thing; the first switch to mainnet asks you to confirm that money there is real. See
[the network toggle](./features/network-toggle.mdx).

## Your first ask

Type or say what you want, the way you'd say it to a person:

> send 1 dollar to @sam

<Clip src="send/chat" alt="Typing “send 2 dollars to @sam” in chat: Kawase understands it, prepares the send and it goes, Signing → Sent → Confirmed." />

Kawase finds the recipient, checks your balance and budget, and shows the payment card: the exact amount, token,
recipient and network. Someone new always waits for your confirmation; inside your budget, a payment to a contact goes
at once. The card follows the payment until the network confirms it, and its receipt links to the explorer. The whole
flow is on [Send, receive and request](./features/send-receive-request.mdx).

Everything else is one tap on **+**: add money, send, receive, swap and bridge.

<Clip src="start/fan-open" alt="Tapping + on Home: Bridge, Swap, Receive, Send and Add money fan out, then Add money opens with every way in." />

Then try:

- "swap 2 dollars to euros"
- "move 1 dollar to Base"
- "put 5 dollars in savings"
- "every Friday at 9 send 1 dollar to Sam"
- "buy 10 dollars of USDC with a card" (Circle's sandbox on testnet)
- "remember that Sam is my brother"

Each one shows a card first and says exactly what it needs from you. On **mainnet** there is more: every token on
Arc, [long and short](./features/long-short.mdx) and borrowing.
