## 1. Open the app

<Tabs items={['Web', 'iPhone', 'Android']}>
  <Tab value="Web">
    Open [app.getkawase.xyz](https://app.getkawase.xyz) in any modern browser. You start as a **guest**: no sign-up, no
    email. You can chat with Kawase, talk to it, look around every screen and see what it would do.

    On the web, an **email code** stands in for Face ID wherever a payment needs your confirmation. You can install the
    web app, or use the [desktop app for Mac](./features/web-and-desktop.mdx).
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="iPhone">
    Kawase for iPhone is distributed through **TestFlight** while it is in beta. Install TestFlight from the App Store,
    then open the invite link you were given. A public TestFlight link will be published on the showcase page when it is
    available.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Android">
    Download the Android build (APK) from Kawase:
    [getkawase.xyz/download](https://getkawase.xyz/download).
    Open it on the phone and allow the install. On Android the confirmation is your **fingerprint or screen lock**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 2. Pick your language

The language pill is on the very first screen. Kawase speaks [ten languages](./features/languages.mdx), and Arabic and
Urdu switch the whole layout to right-to-left at once. You can change it again at any time in Settings.

## 3. Sign in when you want to keep things

Guests can explore everything. To hold money, sign in with:

- an **email code**,
- **Apple**, or
- **Google**.

There is no password and no PIN. Signing in creates your wallet (see [safety](./safety.mdx)): one address that works on
both networks. Everything you did as a guest — your chat, your contacts — moves into your account.

Setting the wallet up costs you nothing: you sign one authorization and Kawase pays the network fee.

## 4. Get test money

New accounts start on **testnet**, where money has no value. Ask Kawase "get me test money" or open the faucet from the
home screen. Each claim brings about **20 test USDC and 20 test EURC** from Circle's faucet, once a day. If the in-app
claim is refused, Circle's own faucet is at [faucet.circle.com](https://faucet.circle.com) (choose "Arc Testnet").

## 5. Accept your daily allowance

Kawase asks you once to accept an **everyday allowance**: an amount of USDC per day that the agent may send for you without
asking again. You approve it with one Face ID. The default is shown on the card before you approve; see the
[limits table](./safety.mdx#default-limits).

## 6. Make your first payment

Type or say, for example (use a friend's @handle, or paste the address of a second wallet of your own):

> send 1 dollar to @friend

Kawase finds the recipient, checks your balance and limits, and shows the payment card: exact amount, currency, recipient
and network. Inside your allowance it sends at once; above it, it waits for your Face ID. The card follows the payment
until the network confirms it, and the receipt links to the block explorer.

## 7. Try the rest

- "swap 2 dollars to euros"
- "move 1 dollar to Base"
- "put 5 dollars in savings"
- "every Friday at 9 send 1 dollar to Alex"
- "keep 20 dollars in my wallet and save the rest"
- "buy 10 dollars of USDC with a card" (Circle's sandbox on testnet)
- "remember that Alex is my brother"

On **mainnet**, Markets, trading any token, long and short, and borrowing are there too. See the [features](./features/swap-and-trade.mdx).

Each one shows a card first and tells you exactly what it needs from you.
