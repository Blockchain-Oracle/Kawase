Kawase (為替, "exchange" in Japanese) is a money app with an AI agent at its centre, built for **Arc**, Circle's stablecoin
network. You tell it what you want — "send 20 dollars to Alex", "swap half my euros to dollars", "every Friday put 10
dollars aside" — by voice or by typing, in any of ten languages. Kawase works out the details, shows you exactly what will
happen, and does it.

## One app for everything on Arc

The goal is that anything a person can do with money on Arc, they can do by asking Kawase:

- **[Send, receive and request](./features/send-receive-request.mdx)** any token, to a saved contact, a Kawase `@handle`,
  any address or a scanned QR code; split a bill with a link per person.
- **[Withdraw](./features/bridge-and-withdraw.mdx#withdraw)** any token to an exchange, your own wallet on another chain,
  or your bank through an exchange, confirmed once.
- **[Trade any token](./features/swap-and-trade.mdx)**: Markets, a page with safety checks for every token, and any pair at
  the best of KyberSwap, Circle's App Kit and LI.FI, meme coins included (mainnet).
- **[Long and short](./features/long-short.mdx)** with Kawase Margin on Aave V4 (mainnet).
- **[Buy](./features/buy.mdx)** with a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Circle's Onramp.
- **[Bridge](./features/bridge-and-withdraw.mdx)** USDC to anyone on six networks over Circle CCTP, Across or Relay, ranked
  by what arrives, and bring USDC to Arc with no gas on the other chain.
- **[Earn and borrow](./features/earn-and-borrow.mdx)**: yield options across Aave V4, Morpho vaults and Aero; borrowing on
  Aave V4 with a health gauge.
- **[Automations](./features/automations.mdx)**: scheduled payments, Auto-save, payroll, DCA and rules, each bounded on chain,
  with practice runs first.
- **[Business](./features/business.mdx)**: invoices with an on-chain reference, batch payouts, payroll.
- **[The agent wallet](./features/marketplace.mdx)**: Kawase pays for services per call over x402, inside a budget.
- **[Limits and control](./features/limits-and-control.mdx)**: your allowance per token, Pause all, practice mode, the exit
  kit and Needs you.
- **[Memory and search](./features/memory-and-search.mdx)**, suggestions on Home, and answers about Arc with sources.
- **Voice** on iPhone, Android and the web, and the whole app in **ten languages**, in the browser or the
  [desktop app](./features/web-and-desktop.mdx).

## Why an agent, and why it is safe

Most money apps make you find the right screen. Kawase lets you say what you mean, and the agent fills in the rest: who
"Alex" is, which currency you hold, which route is cheapest. What it can never do is move money beyond what you allowed:

- **Inside your daily allowance** the agent just sends and tells you — no prompt. The allowance is an on-chain limit you
  signed once; the chain refuses anything above it.
- **Above the allowance**, for a new recipient over a threshold, a look-alike address, a swap or a bridge, you confirm with
  **Face ID** (fingerprint or screen lock on Android, an email code on the web).
- **Automations** run unattended, but each one is pinned on chain to its recipient and amount per period.

Read the [safety model](./safety.mdx) for the details.

## Two networks, one tap

Kawase runs on **Arc mainnet** (real money) and **Arc testnet** (test money, free from the in-app faucet). New accounts
start on testnet. The network pill in the header switches between them in one tap, like dark mode; the app, the orb and
every money card always say which one you are on. See [networks](./networks.mdx).
