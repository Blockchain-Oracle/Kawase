**Activity** (the pulse icon in the dock) is one list of everything that moved: sends and receipts, swaps with both
legs, bridges, savings, positions, automation runs, requests paid, the faucet and practice runs. It reads like a
wallet: money in is **+20 USDC**, money out is **−5 USDC**.

Each row says where it stands: done, moving, held for you, skipped and why, or practice. **Watching** lists what
runs next (a scheduled send, with a ring counting down to it); **Held** is something that waits for you; **Found** is
what Kawase noticed for you, such as money sitting idle.

## The receipt slip

Tap any row. Every transaction opens **the same slip**: a send, a receive, a swap ("You swapped … via KyberSwap") or a
bridge. On a computer the slip opens beside the list instead (see [web and desktop](./web-and-desktop.mdx)).

## Why this happened

**Why** is the decision record behind a movement: the request it came from (your words, an automation, a rule), what
was checked, which rule let it go at once or made it wait, and how you answered. For a run that never reached the
chain, it says what stopped it.

## Money that came in

A receipt for money you **received** says **From** and offers **Save sender**, which saves the address from the payment
as a contact, so the next payment back goes to exactly that address. See
[contacts](./contacts-and-handles.mdx).

## The inbox and the weekly report

The bell on Home is the **inbox**: every payment received, request paid, payment completed and automation skipped lands
there and opens the right receipt ([notifications](./notifications.mdx)). From Activity you can also read **this week's
report** and **export** your history.
