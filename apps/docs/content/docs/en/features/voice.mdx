## Talking to Kawase

Tap the orb in the middle of the dock (**Talk to Kawase** in the sidebar on a computer) and speak. Kawase greets you,
listens, answers aloud and puts the same **cards** on screen as chat does.

- **One conversation.** What you said by voice is in your chat thread, and typing picks it up where you left off.
- **Captions on both sides**: what Kawase heard, and what it is saying.
- **A spoken "yes" never approves a payment.** A send by voice is prepared on its card and waits for your tap and Face
  ID (fingerprint or screen lock on Android, an email code on the web).
- **Type instead** is always one tap away, and **mute** stops anything being heard or sent.

## On the web

Voice works in the web app too: allow the microphone when the browser asks. If you refuse it, or there is no microphone,
Kawase stays connected, says how to allow it, and typing is right beside it. Kawase's own voice still plays.

## How it works

The phone or browser joins a LiveKit room over WebRTC, and Kawase's voice worker joins the same room. Every money or
account request is handed to **the same agent turn as chat** (same tools, same limits, same thread); the cards arrive
on your screen while the answer is spoken. Before speaking amounts, assets, @handles or the network, the worker checks
them against what the tools returned and corrects a mismatch. If the realtime voice can't start, Kawase falls back to a
listen → answer → speak pipeline with the same agent behind it. LiveKit doesn't record the conversation.
