Tap the orb in the dock and speak. The page blurs, the orb rises and listens; it shows the words it heard, answers aloud,
and puts the same **cards** on screen as chat does.

- **One conversation.** What you said by voice is in your chat thread, and typing picks it up where you left off.
- **What was heard is always shown** before anything is confirmed.
- **A spoken "yes" never approves a payment.** A send by voice is prepared on its card and waits for your tap and Face ID
  (fingerprint or screen lock on Android) — even inside your daily allowance.
- **The orb shows the network**: lime on mainnet, sky blue on testnet.

## Languages and voices

Kawase hears all ten languages with ElevenLabs Scribe v2 (realtime). It speaks with ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 in English,
Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic, and with OpenAI's speech model in Bengali and Urdu, which
Flash v2.5 does not cover.

## How it works

The phone joins a LiveKit room over WebRTC. Kawase's voice worker turns speech into text, runs **the same agent turn as
chat** (same tools, same limits, same thread), and speaks the answer while the cards arrive on your screen.
