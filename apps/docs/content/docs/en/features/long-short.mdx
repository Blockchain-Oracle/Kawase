**Kawase Margin** lets you bet on a price going up (**long**) or down (**short**) with more than you put in. It runs on
**Aave V4** on Arc mainnet, through **your own** Aave account: no exchange account, no flash loan, and nothing held by
Kawase. Aave V4 has no testnet, so on testnet the screen explains this and offers the switch.

never confirm numbers you didn't see.

## How a position is built

- **Long X**: your USDC margin is swapped to X and supplied as collateral, then Kawase borrows USDC against it, swaps it
  to more X and supplies that, a few times in a loop.
- **Short X**: your USDC is supplied as collateral, then Kawase borrows X, swaps it to USDC and supplies that, in a loop.
- **Closing** runs it backwards: withdraw, swap, repay, then the rest comes back to your wallet.

The whole position opens or closes as **one transaction** from your own wallet (an EIP-7702 batch). The swap legs come
from KyberSwap with checked calldata. Each loop borrows only down to a safe health at oracle prices, and the account
opens at a **health of 1.10 or more**. Before you slide, the whole batch is **simulated on the chain** from your address;
a position that would fail stops there.

## Markets and leverage

Markets come from Aave V4's live reserves on Arc: **cirBTC** (Circle's Bitcoin) and **WETH**, long and short; **EURC**,
short only, because EURC can't be collateral on Aave V4. Leverage is capped by each asset's collateral factor (today
about **3.4× long cirBTC**, **4.1× long WETH** and **2.4× short**). Margin is paid in **USDC**.

## Your positions

Each position has its own page: **P&L** split into **price** and **borrow interest**, entry and mark price, size,
equity, a **health** gauge and the liquidation price, and **Close 25% · 50% · 75% · All**, which drafts the unwind into
the same ticket ("Slide to close"). Home warns you when an open position's health drops under 1.25 or liquidation is
within a 10% move.
