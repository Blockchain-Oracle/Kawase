**Kawase Margin** lets you bet on a price going up (**long**) or down (**short**) with more than you put in. It runs on
**Aave V4** on Arc mainnet, through **your own** Aave account: no exchange account, no flash loan, and nothing held by
Kawase.

## How a position is built

- **Long X**: your USDC margin is swapped to X and supplied as collateral, then Kawase borrows USDC against it, swaps it to
  more X and supplies that, a few times in a loop.
- **Short X**: your USDC is supplied as collateral, then Kawase borrows X, swaps it to USDC and supplies that, in a loop.
- **Closing** runs it backwards: withdraw, swap, repay, then the rest comes back to your wallet.

The whole position opens or closes as **one transaction** from your own wallet (an EIP-7702 batch). The swap legs come from
KyberSwap with checked calldata. Each loop borrows only down to a safe health at oracle prices, and the account opens at a
**health of 1.10 or more**.

## Markets

Markets come from Aave V4's live reserves on Arc. Today:

- **cirBTC** (Circle's Bitcoin) and **WETH**: long and short;
- **EURC**: short only, because EURC can't be collateral on Aave V4.

Leverage is capped by each asset's collateral factor. At today's factors that is about **3.4× long cirBTC**, **4× long
WETH** and **2.4× for a short**. Margin is paid in **USDC**.

## The ticket

"Long Bitcoin 2x with 10 dollars", or the **Long/Short** screen. Choosing a side drafts a real plan at once ($10, 2×), so
the ticket opens with live numbers:

- your margin and the leveraged size, with presets and Max;
- a **leverage ruler** capped by the market, with a tick at every half step;
- the **liquidation price** and how far away it is, the account's **health**, the borrow rate and the network fee;
- the route, and a risk line.

You confirm by **sliding** (mint for a long, red for a short), then Face ID. The ticket follows the transaction from
checking to final. Any change to the plan resets the slide, so you never confirm numbers you didn't see.

## Positions

Each position has its own page: profit and loss split into **price** and **borrowing cost**, a **health gauge**, its
liquidation price, and **Close 25% · 50% · 75% · All**, which drafts the unwind into the same ticket. Home's **For you**
card warns you when an open position's health drops under 1.25 or liquidation is within a 10% move.

## Not on testnet

Aave V4 is on Arc mainnet only, so Kawase Margin is too. On testnet the screen explains this and offers the switch.
