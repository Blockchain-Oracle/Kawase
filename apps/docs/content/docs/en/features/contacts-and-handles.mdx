## Your @handle

A `@handle` is an optional public name you can be paid by: "send 10 to @maya". You pick it right after signing in
(or skip it), and change it later in **Settings → Handle**.

<Shot src="start/handle" alt="Pick a handle: @ines typed, marked Available, with Continue and Skip for now." zoom="12 47 9" lens="68 58">
  <Pin box="5 37 90 7.5">Checked as you type</Pin>

  <Pin box="5 84 90 7">Keep it, or skip for now</Pin>
</Shot>

- 3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, digits and underscore.
- Unique whatever the case; names that would impersonate Kawase, Arc, Circle or staff are reserved.
- A handle resolves to the person's Kawase wallet. If they haven't set a wallet up yet, Kawase tells you instead of
  guessing.

## Save a contact

A contact is a name (and nicknames) with one or more addresses. Contacts are how Kawase understands "send to Sam", and
how it protects you.

If you mention someone Kawase doesn't know, it asks you to save them and then carries on with the same payment: you
don't start over.

### Import from your phone

**Contacts → Import** reads names and emails **on your phone only**. **Find who's on Kawase** sends only hashed emails,
never the names, emails or numbers themselves; people who allow being found are added by their @handle in one tap, and
you can invite the rest. Whether others can find you this way is a switch on the same page.

## How contacts protect you

- **Address changes are held.** When a contact's address changes, the new address waits **24 hours** before money can
  go to it, and the contact is frozen meanwhile. A changed address is a classic way to steal a payment.
- **Look-alikes are flagged.** An address whose first and last four characters match one of your contacts' is treated
  as a look-alike: Kawase stops and asks you to confirm.
- **History builds trust** per address and per network: a first payment to an address is treated more carefully than a
  repeat one, and can go as a [test amount](./send-receive-request.mdx) first.
