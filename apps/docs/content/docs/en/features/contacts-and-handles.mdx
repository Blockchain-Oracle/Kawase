## Contacts

A contact is a name (and nicknames) with one or more addresses. Kawase uses contacts to understand "send to Alex" and to
protect you:

- **Address changes are held.** When a contact's address changes, the new address waits **24 hours** before money can go to
  it, and the contact is frozen meanwhile. A changed address is a classic way to steal a payment.
- **Look-alike addresses are flagged.** An address whose first and last four characters match one of your contacts' is
  treated as a look-alike: Kawase stops and asks for Face ID.
- **Payment history** builds trust per address and per network: a first payment to an address is treated more carefully
  than a repeat one.

If you mention someone Kawase doesn't know, it asks you to save them and then carries on with the same payment — you don't
have to start over.

### Adding people

- **Save a sender**: money that arrived from someone offers **Save sender as a contact**, with the address from the
  payment.
- **Import from your phone**: Contacts → Import reads names and emails **on your phone only**. **Find who's on Kawase**
  sends only hashed emails, never the names, emails or numbers themselves; people who allow being found are added by their
  @handle in one tap, and you can invite the rest. Whether others can find you this way is a switch on the same page.

## @handles

A `@handle` is an optional public name you can be paid by: "send 10 to @abu". Rules:

- 3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, digits and underscore.
- Unique regardless of case; names that would impersonate Kawase, Arc, Circle or staff are reserved.
- You choose it during onboarding (or skip) and can change it in Settings.

A handle resolves to the person's Kawase wallet address. If the person hasn't set up a wallet yet, Kawase tells you
instead of guessing.
