Settings → **Business** turns Kawase into a small business tool. Your **business profile** (name and details) is printed
on every invoice.

## Invoices

"Invoice Acme 500 USDC for the logo", or **New invoice**: the client, the token (USDC, EURC, any token you hold, or any
token on Arc by name or address; look-alikes are refused), line items, due in 7, 14 or 30 days, and a note, with a live
total. Each invoice gets a number (`INV-0001`, …) and:

- a **payment link** (`/r/…`) to send to the client;
- a **PDF** you can share;
- an **on-chain reference** for Arc's **Memo** contract. It is an opaque id: no names or amounts go on chain.

The client opens the link and sees the invoice: your business, the number, the items, the due date and what is already
paid. They can pay with Kawase, or in any browser with a wallet: **Pay with your wallet** switches it to Arc (adding Arc if
needed) and sends the exact payment the invoice hands over, carrying its reference.

Kawase watches Arc for that reference and records each payment as **partly paid**, **paid** or **overpaid**, with its
transaction. A Kawase send or an exact plain transfer through the link counts too. The invoice page shows the total, what
is paid and what is left, and lets you send the link again, share the PDF or cancel.

## Payouts

Pay many people at once, in any token: paste or type "who, amount" lines (a CSV works), for contacts, @handles or
addresses. Each line is
**checked like a send** (your own address, a token contract, a look-alike, an address on hold) and your balance is
checked. Then **one Face ID** signs **one transaction** that pays everyone: all or nothing. Past payouts list their
transaction.

## Payroll

Name it, pick the day of the month and add the payees. **Turn on** signs every payee's limit with **one Face ID**: each
payee gets their own grant on chain, pinned to them and to their amount per month, so a run can never pay anyone else or
more. Pause, resume or cancel the whole payroll. If a payee's saved address changes, only that line pauses until you
check it; every other line still runs. Each line has its own id, so a run never pays a line twice.

## Reminders

Kawase reminds you the day before an invoice is due, and once when it is overdue: in the inbox and by push, and the
overdue one also by email.
