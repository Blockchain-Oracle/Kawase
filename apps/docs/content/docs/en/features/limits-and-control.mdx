Everything Kawase may do without asking is one **weekly budget**, signed by your wallet and enforced by Arc itself.
Inside it, Kawase acts without asking each time; anything else waits for you. The model behind it is on the
[safety page](../safety.mdx).

## Your weekly budget

You turn it on right after setting up your wallet ([getting started](../getting-started.mdx)); afterwards it lives in
**Settings → Weekly budget**.

Lowering a number saves at once. Raising one shows you the change and asks your wallet to sign the new budget, which
also switches off the old one: two budgets are never live at the same time. **Hold to revoke everything** takes every
permission back on Arc in one transaction.

## What always asks

Even inside the budget, these wait for you, with the reason on the card and the fix beside it:

- a **first payment** to someone, or a **new destination**;
- **opening a position**, or anything **above your limit per action** or **more than what's left this week**;
- a category or venue that is **not part of your budget**;
- a token Kawase doesn't vouch for, a price it **couldn't check**, or one **worse than the market price**;
- anything while Kawase is **paused**, in **Guard** mode or in **practice**.

Approving once covers that one action; the budget itself only changes when you change it.

## Practice mode

With practice on, Kawase runs **the exact transaction** you asked for as a dry run on Arc, from your own address, and
shows **"Would have sent"** with the chain's answer. **Nothing moves.** Automations run in practice too, and a
marketplace purchase answers "would pay" with the live price.

## Token rules

**Token rules** set which tokens Kawase may buy for you, with caps for unverified tokens per trade and per day and the
highest price impact you accept; see [Trade any token](./swap-and-trade.mdx#token-rules).

## Needs you

Some steps an automation may never take alone: **every swap**, a payment above its grant, or one your rules hold back.
They wait in **Needs you**, in the inbox and in Automations, with a push notification where notifications are on.
Answer from anywhere, once; approving opens the very send or swap you would make by hand, re-checked. Unanswered items
are reminded after **12 hours** and expire after **24**.

## The exit kit

If something feels wrong: **Pause everything**, then **hold to revoke** every permission, then **Move my money out**,
which opens [Withdraw](./bridge-and-withdraw.mdx#withdraw) for everything. It sits at the bottom of Withdraw.

## Activity and why

Every movement is in [Activity](./activity-and-receipts.mdx), and every receipt has **Why this happened**: the request
it came from, what was checked, and which rule let it go or made it wait.

## From chat

Ask "what are my limits?", "pause everything", "set the dial to guard" or "revoke everything". Each answer is a card,
and changes are approved on the card.
