Everything the agent may do without asking is written down in **Settings → Wallet & limits**, and most of it is enforced
by Arc itself. This page shows what you can change there. The model behind it is on the [safety page](../safety.mdx).

## Your allowance, per token

The **authority receipt** shows, for each token, what Arc enforces (the grant you signed) and Kawase's own lower cap, what
is left today, and any temporary raise.

- **Add a token**: any token you hold, or an address you paste, checked on chain.
- **Lower** a number and it saves at once.
- **Raise** one and you see **Now → After**: one approval signs the new on-chain grant **together with** the revoke of the
  old one, so two grants are never live at the same time.

## What Kawase checks on its own

On top of the chain's limit, Kawase applies rules of its own:

- a **per-payment** cap;
- **new recipients**: for the first hours after you add someone, the allowance and automations stay off that address;
  your own payment still goes through, with a test amount suggested;
- **allowlist only**: the agent pays only people on your list;
- a **temporary raise**: more for a while, then back by itself.

## The brake

Some requests are unusual enough to stop and show you why, even inside your limits: **more than half your balance to
someone new**, a trade whose **price impact** is over your rule, or **leverage above 5×**. The card shows a brake panel;
it's your call, and approving goes ahead. An automation that hits the brake waits in Needs you instead.

## Token rules

**Wallet & limits → Token rules** sets which tokens Kawase may buy for you: **verified only**, or verified and popular
ones with warnings; caps for unverified tokens per trade and per day, with today's meter; and the highest price impact you
accept. Copycats and high-risk tokens are always blocked. Tightening applies at once; loosening asks for Face ID and your
wallet's signature.

## Pause all

One switch turns off everything the agent does **without asking**: no payment uses the allowance (each one waits for your
Face ID), every automation run is skipped, and the agent buys nothing in the marketplace. What you do yourself still
works, swaps, bridges and withdrawals included, so you can always move your money out. While paused, a strip sits above
every screen.

## The risk dial

**Guard · Balanced · Autonomous**:

- **Guard**: every payment waits for your confirmation, chat too.
- **Balanced**: inside your allowance, a typed request goes at once.
- **Autonomous**: Kawase may act on its own suggestions inside your limits, then tells you.

## Practice mode

With practice on, Kawase runs **the exact transaction** you asked for as a dry run on Arc, from your own address, and shows
**"Would have sent"** with the chain's answer. **Nothing moves.** Practice also covers automations, and a marketplace
purchase answers **"would pay"** with the live price and nothing paid. Swaps, bridges, savings moves and margin orders are
refused before anything is signed, so the PRACTICE strip never hides a real payment.

## The exit kit

If something feels wrong: **pause**, then **hold to revoke** every permission on the network with **one Face ID** (one
transaction), then **move the money out**, which opens [Withdraw](./bridge-and-withdraw.mdx#withdraw) for everything.
Automations whose grants are revoked end.

## Needs you

Some steps an automation may never take alone: **every swap**, a payment **above its grant**, or one your **allowlist** or
**cooldown** holds back. They wait in **Needs you**, in the inbox and in Automations, with a push notification where
notifications are set up. Answer from anywhere, once: a second answer finds it already answered and says when and where.
Approving opens the very send or swap you would make by hand, re-checked. Unanswered items are reminded after **12
hours** and expire after **24**.

## Activity and why

**Activity** is one feed of every token in and out: sends, receives, swaps with both legs, bridges, savings, margin,
automation runs, requests paid, the faucet and practice runs. Each row says where it stands (checking, skipped and why,
practice, waiting). Filters: **All · In · Out · Trades · Automations · Needs you**, the last with its count.

Tap a row for its receipt (amount, time, network, the other side, the transaction), then **Why**: the decision record
behind it, or for a run that never reached the chain, what stopped it and how you answered. From Activity you can also
read **the week's report** and **export** your history as CSV or beancount.

## From chat

Ask "what are my limits?", "pause everything", "set the dial to guard", "raise my USDC allowance to 50" or "revoke
everything". Each answer is a card, and changes are approved on the card.
