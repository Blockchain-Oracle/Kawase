## Memory

"Remember that my landlord is @maria." Kawase keeps **facts you ask it to remember** (people, @handles, preferences) and
uses them later. When an answer relies on one, chat shows a **From memory** line, so you always know where it came from.

**Settings → Memory** lists every fact: add one, edit it, forget one or all, or turn memory off. A guest's memory moves
into the account on sign-in. Kawase doesn't remember things you didn't ask it to.

## Search every conversation

The history sheet searches **all** your conversations on Kawase's server, not just the ones loaded: the words, the cards
and who they were for (search "Maria" and the send to her shows up). Partial words and Chinese, Japanese or Korean text
work. Results are grouped by conversation with the words around the match, and open the conversation. You can also ask:
"what did I send my landlord last month?"

## Long conversations

A long thread stays coherent: after each turn, the older part is folded into a summary and the latest messages are kept
word for word. The summary never restates amounts or outcomes from memory; the cards' own results are kept exactly as they
were, and Kawase checks again with its tools before relying on one.

## For you

**Home** shows a few cards that deserve a look, computed from your real balances on the active network:

- idle USDC above what you spend, next to a vault that could pay it all back;
- one of your payment requests about to expire;
- a bridge that hasn't arrived 15 minutes after it should have;
- a big move in a token you hold (5% in a day, 0.5% for a currency stablecoin, at least $10 held);
- a [margin position](./long-short.mdx) whose health is under 1.25 or within a 10% move of liquidation.

They're facts and options, never advice. Each card has one action. **Dismiss** hides a card for a week; **don't show this
kind** mutes it (and can be undone).

## Answers about Arc, with sources

Ask "how fast is finality on Arc?" or "what does a transfer cost?". Kawase answers from a curated set of notes built from
Arc's and Circle's documentation and these docs, and shows a **Sources** card naming each one. Facts only, no investment
advice.

## Cards that explain

- **Charts**: your portfolio's value, or any token's price, over a day, a week, a month or a year.
- **Why this one?**: why a bridge route, swap venue or vault won (every provider asked, what each delivers, the fee and the
  time, why the others lost or couldn't quote).
- **Settings changes** as Now → After, applied on your device (memory on or off, reply language, theme).
- **Status**: whether Kawase, Arc (each network), Circle and prices are up, slow or down.

## Outside text is data

Anything Kawase reads from outside (marketplace answers, token and vault names, chain memos, contact notes, past messages)
is fenced as untrusted. Instructions hidden in it are flagged and never followed.
