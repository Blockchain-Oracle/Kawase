Ask Kawase ("send 20 dollars to Alex on Base", "bring my USDC from Base to Arc") and it puts a **move card** in front of
you, which also opens full screen as **Move to another chain**. It moves USDC between Arc and **Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum,
Optimism, Polygon or Avalanche**.

## Out of Arc, to anyone

The recipient can be **you**, a **contact**, an **@handle** or any **0x address**. Kawase asks three providers for a live
quote:

- **Circle CCTP**: Circle burns the USDC on Arc and mints it on the other network;
- **Across** and **Relay**: a solver pays out on the other side, and is repaid from your deposit.

They are ranked by **what arrives after fees**, with ties going to the faster one. Up to three routes are shown with the
provider's mark, what arrives, the fee and the expected time. The best one is picked for you, and you can tap another.

Before your wallet is asked, Kawase builds the chosen provider's transaction and **checks it**: for Across it decodes the
deposit (who pays, who receives, which token, the amount, the minimum, the destination, no extra message); for Relay it
checks the approval and deposit into Relay's listed depository; for CCTP, Circle's own call checks. The transaction is
simulated from your wallet, then sent as **one transaction** after you slide and confirm with Face ID.

## Bring to Arc

If you hold USDC at the same address on another network, Kawase finds it (the balances are shown, the largest picked) and
brings it to Arc with **Relay's permit**: one signature, and **no gas needed on that network**. The permit is checked
(your address, the amount, Relay's contract and that network's USDC) before you sign.

## Following a move

Every move is tracked: **Signed → Sent → Arriving → Delivered**, with explorer links. Kawase checks the amount that left
your wallet on the receipt, then the fill through the provider's own status (Across deposit status, Relay request status
or Circle's attestation). If a provider **refunds** instead, the card says so plainly. Nothing is ever sent twice.

## On testnet

Arc Testnet uses **CCTP only**; Across and Relay serve Arc mainnet. Bring to Arc is mainnet only.

## Withdraw

**Withdraw** (from Home, or "withdraw everything to my Kraken") takes one token, several or everything out of Kawase:

<Steps>
<Step>
### What
Pick the tokens. Each one goes **as itself** or **as cash** (converted to USDC first).
</Step>
<Step>
### Where
An **exchange** (Kraken, Bybit, OKX, Binance, Bitget, KuCoin, or Coinbase through Base), **your own wallet** on Arc or
another chain, or **your bank** through an exchange. Kawase knows which assets and networks each exchange accepts and
warns you before you send something it would not credit. Places you withdraw to are saved in **My places**.
</Step>
<Step>
### One confirmation
Kawase plans a line per token: a transfer on Arc, a bridge, or a conversion first. You slide once and confirm with one
Face ID. A tracker follows every line to the end; if something stops half way, **Finish it** picks up where it was.
</Step>
</Steps>

Tokens other than USDC cross chains as USDC (converted first). On testnet, exchanges aren't offered; withdraw goes to your
own wallet. In the **exit kit**, "move the money out" opens Withdraw for everything.

To take your money out without Kawase at all, follow [Withdraw without Kawase](../withdraw-without-kawase.mdx).
