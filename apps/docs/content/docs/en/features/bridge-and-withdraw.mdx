## Bridge, step by step

**Bridge** from **+** (or Swap's Bridge tab), or ask: "send 20 dollars to Alex on Base", "move 1 dollar to Base".

<Walkthrough>
  <WalkStep title="Choose a chain" src="bridge/chains">
    Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon or Avalanche, each with its own mark.

    <Pin box="4 72.5 92 24">Where it should arrive</Pin>
  </WalkStep>
  <WalkStep title="To whom, and how much" src="bridge/ticket">
    The recipient can be **you** (the same address on the other chain), a **contact**, an **@handle** or any
    **0x address**. Pick USDC or EURC and the amount.

    <Pin box="4 16.5 41 8">From Arc</Pin>

    <Pin box="55 16.5 41 8">To the chain you chose</Pin>

    <Pin box="5 29 90 6.5">You, a contact, an @handle or an address</Pin>

    <Pin box="4 45.5 40 5">What to move</Pin>
  </WalkStep>
  <WalkStep title="The routes" src="bridge/review">
    **Circle CCTP, Across, Relay, LI.FI, deBridge, Socket and Gas.zip** are asked for a live quote. They are ranked by
    **what arrives after fees**, ties going to the faster one; the best is picked and you can tap another. Before your
    wallet is asked, Kawase builds the chosen route and **checks it** (who pays, who receives, the token, the amount,
    the minimum, the destination), then simulates it from your wallet.

    <Pin at="60 15.3">What arrives, on which chain</Pin>

    <Pin box="5 28 90 13">The winning bridge, what you receive, its fee</Pin>

    <Pin box="5 66.5 90 7.5">Slide to send</Pin>
  </WalkStep>
  <WalkStep title="Delivered" src="bridge/tracking">
    Every move is followed: **Signed → Sent → Arriving → Delivered**, with explorer links on both chains. Kawase checks
    the amount that left your wallet, then the fill through the bridge's own status (Circle's attestation, Across's
    deposit status, Relay's request status). If a bridge **refunds** instead, the card says so. Nothing is sent twice.

    <Pin box="8 21.5 84 9">What arrived, and by which bridge</Pin>

    <Pin box="8 30.5 84 12">See it on both chains</Pin>
  </WalkStep>
</Walkthrough>

**Their wallet is on another chain?** The [Send](./send-receive-request.mdx) review offers the bridge with the same
amount and recipient.

## Bring USDC to Arc

If you hold USDC at the same address on another network, **Add money → From another chain** finds it (the largest
balance first) and brings it to Arc with **Relay's permit**: one signature and **no gas needed on that network**. The
permit is checked (your address, the amount, Relay's contract and that network's USDC) before you sign. Mainnet only.

## Withdraw

**Withdraw** takes one token, several or everything out of Kawase, in one confirmation.

<Walkthrough>
  <WalkStep title="What, and where" src="bridge/withdraw">
    Pick tokens or tap **Everything**; each goes **as itself** or **as cash** (converted to USDC first). Then where:
    an **exchange** (Kraken, Bybit, OKX, Binance, Bitget, KuCoin, or Coinbase through Base), **your other wallet** on
    Arc or another chain, or **your bank** through an exchange. Kawase knows which assets and networks each exchange
    accepts and warns you before you send something it would not credit.

    <Pin at="67 34.8">Everything, or pick tokens</Pin>

    <Pin box="4 50.5 92 22.5">Exchanges, with their own steps</Pin>

    <Pin box="4 79.5 92 7.5">Your other wallet, on any chain</Pin>

    <Pin box="4 87.5 92 7">Your bank, through an exchange</Pin>
  </WalkStep>
  <WalkStep title="One confirmation, every line" src="limits/exit-kit">
    Kawase plans a line per token (a transfer on Arc, a bridge, or a conversion first). **One Face ID covers every
    line**, and each step is checked again before it is sent. A tracker follows every line; if something stops half
    way, **Finish it** picks up where it was. The **exit kit** sits under it: pause Kawase, take every permission back,
    then move your money out.

    <Pin box="4 57.5 92 14">The exit kit</Pin>

    <Pin box="4 75.5 92 6">Pause everything Kawase does on its own</Pin>

    <Pin box="4 87.5 92 6.5">Move my money out: Withdraw, for everything</Pin>
  </WalkStep>
</Walkthrough>

Places you withdraw to are saved in **Contacts → My places**. Exchanges hold real money, so they open on Arc mainnet;
on testnet, withdraw goes to your own wallet. To take your money out without Kawase at all, follow
[Withdraw without Kawase](../withdraw-without-kawase.mdx).

## On testnet

Arc Testnet bridges with **Circle CCTP** (the others answer that they aren't on that network), to the testnets of the
same six chains. Bring USDC to Arc and exchanges are mainnet only.
