## Bridge USDC out of Arc (today)

"Move 20 dollars to Base" or the Bridge screen. Kawase moves **USDC from Arc to your own address** on **Base, Ethereum,
Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon or Avalanche** through **Circle CCTP** with Circle's forwarder:

- Your USDC is burned on Arc and minted on the destination network by Circle; you need no gas on the other side. The
  fee comes out of the amount and is shown before you confirm.
- It takes about a minute. The card follows three steps: the burn on Arc, Circle's attestation, and the mint on the
  destination network — and it checks the money actually arrived at your address there.
- You confirm with **one Face ID**. The approval and the burn go as one transaction from your wallet. The fee is capped:
  if the route's fee rises above the cap you were shown, nothing is sent.

## Routes compared

Before you confirm, Kawase can compare the same transfer across providers and rank them by what actually arrives:

- **Circle CCTP** — the route Kawase executes today.
- **LI.FI** (both networks), **Relay** and **deBridge** (mainnet) — quoted for comparison only today.
- **Across** and **Relay** as executed routes, and bridging **into** Arc, are being added.

## Withdraw anywhere (coming)

Today you can already send USDC or EURC to **any address on Arc** — including an exchange's Arc deposit address — and
bridge USDC to **your own** address on the six networks above. Withdrawing to any address on another network (a friend,
an exchange elsewhere) in one step is **coming**.

If you ever need to leave without Kawase, see [Withdraw without Kawase](../withdraw-without-kawase.mdx).
