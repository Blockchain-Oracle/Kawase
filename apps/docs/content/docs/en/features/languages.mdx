title: Ten languages
description: The whole app, the agent's replies and voice in ten languages, switchable from the first screen, with right-to-left layouts for Arabic and Urdu.
---

Kawase speaks the world's most widely spoken languages, everywhere in the app:

| Language | Name in the language | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| English | English | left to right |
| Chinese | 中文 | left to right |
| Hindi | हिन्दी | left to right |
| Spanish | Español | left to right |
| French | Français | left to right |
| Arabic | العربية | **right to left** |
| Bengali | বাংলা | left to right |
| Portuguese | Português | left to right |
| Russian | Русский | left to right |
| Urdu | اردو | **right to left** |

- **From the first screen.** The language pill is on the first onboarding panel and every screen after it; changing it is
  instant, direction included.
- **Every string in every language.** The app's ten catalogs are complete; the agent replies in your language and
  [voice](./voice.mdx) hears and speaks all ten.
- **Amounts stay exact.** Numbers are formatted for your language, while amounts, addresses and codes stay left-to-right
  so they can be checked character by character.
- **These docs** are available in the same ten languages (use the language switch in the sidebar).
