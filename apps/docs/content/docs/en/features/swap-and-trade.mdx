## The route race

Type an amount and Kawase asks **KyberSwap, Circle App Kit, LI.FI, Uniswap, 0x and Socket** at the same moment. Each
answer lands as it comes; the one that delivers the most **after fees** is picked, and every other venue is listed with
what it would deliver, or why it couldn't quote. Guests see the race too: you only sign in at the end.

## Your portfolio, every token

Home shows **every token your address holds** on Arc and on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP, Polygon and Avalanche, each
with its real mark and value. An **impostor** (a well-known symbol at another address, or with other decimals) is
hidden; a token with **no price and no pool** goes to Hidden; an unpriced token never counts toward your total.

## Token rules

Every buy passes your **token rules** first (Settings → Budget → Token rules):

- copycats, honeypots and tokens that can't be sold are **never** bought, whatever the rules say;
- **which tokens**: verified only, or verified and popular with warnings;
- caps for **unverified** tokens per trade and per day, with today's meter;
- the **price impact** you accept (1% to 15%; a swap that would lose more is refused).

Tightening a rule applies at once; loosening one asks for Face ID, and your wallet signs the new rules.

## On testnet

Arc Testnet has **USDC ↔ EURC** to trade, through the same ticket and the same race (Circle App Kit quotes it; the
others say they aren't on that network). Every other token, and meme coins, are mainnet only.
