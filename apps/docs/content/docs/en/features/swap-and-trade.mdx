## Markets

The **Markets** tab (in the dock, between Home and the orb) is open to guests too:

- **Search** by name, symbol or contract address.
- Segments **Trending · Meme · Long/Short · Stocks & FX · New**, a carousel of hot markets, and a **heatmap** where each
  tile is sized by the day's volume and coloured by its move.
- Rows show the price, the day's change, volume and liquidity. A token that copies another one carries a **copycat**
  chip; fakes stay flagged in the list and never reach the carousel or the heatmap.

Markets are an Arc mainnet thing. On testnet the tab says so and offers the switch.

## A page for every token

Tap any token, in Markets, on Home or in a chat card, to open its page:

- its mark, price and change, and a chart you can scrub with ranges from one hour to all time;
- **what you hold** of it;
- market cap, liquidity, volume, holders, fully diluted value and the contract (copy it in one tap);
- **Safety**: a verdict with its reasons, the checks from **GoPlus** (honeypot, buy and sell taxes, owner powers) and any
  look-alikes, linked;
- **Buy · Sell**, and a **More** sheet that lists every other action, each with the reason when it isn't available.

## Your portfolio, every token

Home shows **every token your address holds** on Arc and on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP, Polygon and Avalanche, each with
its real mark and value. Kawase hides what would mislead you:

- an **impostor** (a stablecoin's or a well-known symbol at another address, or with other decimals) is hidden;
- a token with **no price and no pool** goes to Hidden;
- an unpriced token never counts toward your total.

## Buy, sell or swap any pair

"Buy 20 dollars of ARGUS", "sell half my cirBTC", or the ticket from a token page. You pay with **any token you hold**
and get any token (search by name, symbol or address). For every trade Kawase asks **three venues at once**, KyberSwap,
Circle's App Kit and LI.FI, and recommends the one that delivers the most. The others are listed with what they would
deliver, or why they couldn't quote.

The review shows the amount you get **at least**, the price impact against the market price and the route. You confirm by
**sliding** on the card, then **Face ID**. Only the chosen venue builds the transaction, at half the slippage you were
shown, and Kawase checks its calls (router, amounts, recipient, the on-chain minimum) before your wallet is asked. The
approval and the trade go out as **one transaction** from your own wallet.

## Token rules

Every buy passes your **token rules** first:

- impersonators and honeypots are **blocked**;
- an **unverified** token is limited to **$50 a trade** and **$200 a day**;
- price impact may be at most **5%** for anything that isn't a stablecoin.

A blocked trade says which rule stopped it and what would change the answer.

## On testnet

Arc Testnet has only **USDC ↔ EURC** to trade, through the same ticket. Every other token, and meme coins, are mainnet
only.
