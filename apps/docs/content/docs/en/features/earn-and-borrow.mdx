Your **positions** are read live from the vaults. A vault that couldn't give your money back now is never offered, and
low liquidity is flagged. On testnet, Earn uses Arc Testnet's test vaults with test money.

### Where it can earn

"Where can my 200 dollars earn?" compares every source that takes your asset, from **live reads**: **Aave V4** supply
(the rate, how much the market could pay out now, its size), **Morpho vaults** listed by Circle's App Kit Earn, and
**Aero** liquidity campaigns from Merkl (shown as advanced, read only). They are ranked by rate among the sources that
could return your amount.

## Auto-save

"Keep 100 dollars in my wallet and save the rest." **Auto-save** keeps that amount ready and moves anything above it into
your own vault savings; when your wallet drops below the line, it tops it back up. It checks daily and whenever money
arrives, and its permission only lets it move USDC **between your wallet and your own savings**. See
[automations](./automations.mdx).

## Borrow and supply on Aave V4

**Borrow** (mainnet) shows your Aave V4 position first: what you owe (with **Repay**), what you supplied (with **Add**
and **Withdraw**), the **health gauge** and how much room is left; then what you can borrow, and at what rate.

- **Borrow** USDC, EURC, cirBTC or WETH against **USDC, cirBTC or WETH**. EURC can't be collateral on Aave V4. Kawase
  picks the collateral from your wallet and supplies it **in the same transaction**.
- The gauge moves with every digit you type; **Max** keeps you at the safe floor. A loan that would leave health
  **under 1.25 is refused**, and **under 1.5** you're warned. With one collateral asset, the ticket shows the price at
  which it would be liquidated.
- **Repay** part or all. "All" repays everything with a small margin for interest, and resets the approval.

Every action is re-checked against fresh prices when you approve, simulated from your wallet, and sent as one
transaction you confirm by sliding. Aave V4 is on Arc mainnet only; Morpho vaults work on both networks.
