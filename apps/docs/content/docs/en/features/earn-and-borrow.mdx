## Yield options

"Where can my 200 dollars earn?" Kawase compares every source that takes your asset, from **live reads**:

- **Aave V4** supply: the current rate, how much the market could pay out right now, and its size;
- **Morpho vaults** listed by Circle's App Kit Earn, ranked by what they could give back now. A vault that couldn't return
  your money is never offered, and low liquidity is flagged;
- **Aero** liquidity campaigns from Merkl: shown as **advanced**, read only.

Options are ranked by rate among the sources that could return your amount, with the best one marked. Tapping Aave opens
the supply ticket; tapping a vault opens Earn for that vault.

## Earn with Morpho vaults

"Put 50 dollars in savings", or the **Earn** screen. Deposit USDC into the vault you pick (or the best active one) and
withdraw any amount back. Your **positions** are read live from the vaults. Each deposit or withdrawal is **one Face ID**,
sent as one transaction. [Auto-save](./automations.mdx#auto-save) uses the same vaults by itself.

On testnet, Earn uses Arc Testnet's mock Morpho vaults.

## Borrow and supply on Aave V4

The **Borrow** screen (from Earn on mainnet) shows your Aave V4 position first: what you owe (with **Repay**), what you
supplied (with **Add** and **Withdraw**), the **health gauge** and how much room is left. Then what you can borrow, and at
what rate.

- **Borrow** USDC, EURC, cirBTC or WETH against collateral of **USDC, cirBTC or WETH**. EURC can't be collateral on Aave V4.
  Kawase picks the collateral from your wallet (the held asset worth most after its factor) and puts it in **in the same
  transaction**.
- The gauge moves with every digit you type. Max keeps you at the safe floor.
- A loan that would leave health **under 1.25 is refused**; **under 1.5** you're warned.
- With one collateral asset, the ticket shows the **price at which it would be liquidated**.
- **Repay** part or all. "All" repays everything with a small margin for interest, and resets the approval.

Every action is re-checked against fresh prices when you approve, simulated from your wallet, and sent as **one
transaction**. You confirm by sliding and Face ID.

## Not on testnet

Aave V4 is on Arc mainnet only, so borrowing and Aave supply are too. Morpho vaults work on both networks.
