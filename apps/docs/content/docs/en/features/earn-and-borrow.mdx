## Earn with Morpho vaults (today)

"Put 50 dollars in savings" or the Earn screen. Kawase lists the **Morpho vaults** available through **Circle's App Kit
Earn** on Arc (on testnet: Arc Testnet's mock vaults) with readable names and their rates, and:

- **Deposits** USDC into the vault you pick — or the best active one when you don't say — with a minimum number of vault
  shares checked against the vault's own preview.
- **Withdraws** any amount back to your wallet, paying at least the amount shown.
- Shows your **positions**, read live from the vaults on chain (never stored by Kawase).

Each deposit or withdrawal is **one Face ID**, sent as one transaction. [Auto-save](./automations.mdx#auto-save) uses the
same vaults to keep your savings topped up by itself.

## More yield and borrowing (coming)

The plan compares every source that accepts your asset — **Aave V4**, Morpho vaults directly and others — showing the
rate, how much can be withdrawn right now, size and risk, and hides sources you couldn't exit. **Borrowing** USDC or EURC
against collateral (for example on Aave V4) is planned with a health gauge, repay and add-collateral actions, and
liquidation alerts in your inbox. Both are **coming**.
