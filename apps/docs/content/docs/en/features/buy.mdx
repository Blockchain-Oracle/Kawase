Buying is planned through **Circle's Onramp Kit**: pay with a debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay and receive USDC or EURC
directly in your Kawase wallet on Arc. The session is created by Kawase's server and opened in a secure browser sheet;
Kawase marks the purchase complete only when the money is confirmed on chain.

To buy any other token with Apple Pay, the plan is to buy USDC first and then swap in one approval, with both fees shown
before you pay.

This is **coming**: it is not available in the app yet. Until it is, on testnet you can use the free
[faucet](../getting-started.mdx#4-get-test-money); on mainnet you can receive USDC from any wallet or exchange that sends to
Arc.
