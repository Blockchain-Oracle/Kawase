## Checkout and delivery

The provider's checkout opens **inside Kawase** (on the web too), with its rail on top: **Paying → Buying → On Arc**
(Coinbase adds **Moving to Arc**). Card and identity checks are the provider's, never Kawase's. It is safe to leave:
Kawase follows the order until **the chain confirms the delivery**, then says "You're funded". If the payment didn't
complete, the provider's reason is shown with **Try again**, and it always says whether any money moved.

## Buying another token

Ask for any other token ("buy 30 dollars of cirBTC with Apple Pay") and Kawase buys USDC first, then opens the
[swap](./swap-and-trade.mdx) for what arrived, with one more approval and both fees shown. It only ever continues to a
canonical or listed token, never a look-alike.

## Where it runs

- **Mainnet**: real money, with the provider's own limits and checks.
- **Testnet**: Circle's sandbox with test cards; no real money moves. The free test USDC in Add money works too.

Selling to a bank account goes through an exchange: see [Withdraw](./bridge-and-withdraw.mdx#withdraw).
