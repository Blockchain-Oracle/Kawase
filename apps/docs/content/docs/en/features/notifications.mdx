## The inbox

The bell on Home opens the **inbox**: every payment received, request paid, payment completed, automation skipped and
rule alert, newest first, each opening its receipt. **Mark all read** clears the dot.

Per event and per channel, you choose what reaches you: **money received**, **someone asks you to pay**, **a request was
paid**, **a send went through**, **an automation was skipped**, **a rule's alert**, **new sign-ins**, **devices signed
out**, **health alerts** for open positions, **take-profit and stop-loss**, and **token graduations**.

- **On your phone**: push, and email.
- **On the web**: the inbox and email (the web app has no push).
- **Quiet hours** in your own time zone hold push until they end; your inbox and email still get everything.

Email is starting soon: the settings screen says so, and your choices are kept until it does. Kawase's emails carry
the same [receipt slip](./activity-and-receipts.mdx) as the app. Each notification is delivered at most once per
channel, even if Kawase retries.
