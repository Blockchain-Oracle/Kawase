---
title: Notifications
description: An in-app inbox for everything that happens to your money, with push and email for the events you choose.
---

Kawase's **inbox** is the record: every payment received, request paid, payment completed, automation skipped and rule
alert lands there, and tapping one opens the right screen.

On top of the inbox, Kawase can tell you by **push** (on your phone) and **email**. In Settings → Notifications you choose,
per event and per channel, what reaches you. The defaults:

| Event | Push | Email |
|---|---|---|
| Money received | on | on |
| Your request was paid | on | on |
| Your payment completed | on | off |
| An automation was skipped | on | on |
| A rule alert | on | on |

**Quiet hours** in your own time zone hold push notifications until morning; email still goes. Security events can never
be muted.

Each notification is delivered at most once per channel, even if Kawase retries. Push and email delivery are built into
the app and the server; they switch on as the push (Apple and Google) and email provider accounts are connected for the
public release. The inbox works today.
