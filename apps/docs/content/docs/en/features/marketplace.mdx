**x402** is an open standard for paying for a web request with stablecoins: the service answers "402 Payment Required"
with a price, the client pays in USDC and gets the result. Kawase's plan is to let the agent **find and pay for services**
from Circle's x402 catalog on your behalf, strictly inside a budget:

- a separate marketplace wallet whose balance is the hard cap,
- limits per call, per session and per day, set by you,
- every purchase shown with its price, the service and the result.

Circle's catalog lists mainnet services only, so the marketplace is planned for **mainnet** and is not on testnet. It is
**coming**: not available in the app yet.
