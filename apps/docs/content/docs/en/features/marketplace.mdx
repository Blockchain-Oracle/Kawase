**x402** is an open standard for paying for a web request with stablecoins: the service answers "402 Payment Required"
with a price, the client pays in USDC and gets the result. Kawase uses it to **buy answers for you**: web search and news,
prediction-market odds, DefiLlama yields and prices, stock prices, data enrichment, from **Circle's x402 catalog** on Arc.

## Your agent wallet

Purchases never come out of your main wallet directly. Kawase has a separate **agent wallet** for you, and it can only
spend what you put in:

- **Top up** from your Kawase wallet with a preset. The first top-up signs a **capped marketplace grant** with Face ID;
  later top-ups need no prompt, inside that cap.
- Its balance is split between **Circle Gateway** (ready for nanopayments, which most sellers take) and a part **on hand**
  for sellers that take plain USDC (EIP-3009). When a plain-USDC purchase needs more on hand, Kawase refills it from
  Gateway itself.
- **Return to my wallet** sends both parts back at any time.

Settings → **Agent wallet** shows the balance, what was spent today, the address and the full history of purchases and
moves.

## Budgets you set

- a **daily budget** and a **per-call cap**;
- **ask me first** above the cap: the purchase waits on a consent card in chat;
- the **categories** Kawase may buy from;
- **blocked sellers**: tap any purchase to block its seller.

Tightening applies at once; loosening asks for Face ID. A refusal names the rule that stopped it.

## Every purchase receipted

Chat shows what Kawase found (the seller's mark, what it returns and the price) and, after paying, a receipt: "Paid 0.002
USDC to …". The seller's answer is labelled **unchecked**. Kawase treats outside text as data, never as instructions, and
says so when an answer tries to tell it what to do.

## Networks

- **Mainnet**: Circle's catalog (about 2,100 listings on Arc), paid over both rails.
- **Testnet**: Circle's catalog has no testnet listings, so Kawase runs its own demo seller (`seller.getkawase.xyz`) to
  try it end to end with test money.
