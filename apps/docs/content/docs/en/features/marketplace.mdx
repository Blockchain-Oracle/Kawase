**x402** is an open standard for paying for a web request with stablecoins: the service answers "402 Payment Required"
with a price, the client pays in USDC and gets the result. Kawase uses it to **buy answers for you**: web search and
news, prediction-market odds, DefiLlama yields and prices, stock prices, data enrichment, from **Circle's x402
catalog** on Arc.

## The marketplace

**Markets → Marketplace** (in the sidebar on a computer) lists every paid tool your agent can call: about 84 on Arc
mainnet, by category, each with its seller's mark, its price per call and how many calls a dollar buys.

## Your agent wallet

Purchases never come out of your main wallet directly. Kawase keeps a separate **agent wallet** for you, and it can
only spend what you put in.

- **Top up** from your Kawase wallet with a preset. The first top-up asks you to confirm once: Kawase may then move USDC
  only into your agent wallet, with a daily limit. Or top up with **Apple Pay or a card**, straight into it.
- Its balance is split between **Circle Gateway** (ready for nanopayments, which most sellers take) and a part **on
  hand** for sellers that take plain USDC. Kawase refills what's on hand from Gateway itself.
- **Return to my wallet** sends both parts back at any time.

## Budgets you set

- a **daily budget** and a **per-call cap**;
- **ask me before every purchase**, or only above the cap, on a consent card in chat;
- the **categories** Kawase may buy from;
- **blocked sellers**: tap any purchase to block its seller.

Tightening applies at once; loosening asks you to confirm. A refusal names the rule that stopped it.

## Every purchase receipted

Chat shows what Kawase found (the seller's mark, what it returns, the price) and, after paying, a receipt: "Paid 0.002
USDC to …". The seller's answer is labelled **unchecked**. x402 has no refund standard yet: if a call fails after
payment, the receipt says the seller kept it; a payment that Circle reports as failed is marked **not charged**. Kawase
treats outside text as data, never as instructions.

## Networks

- **Mainnet**: Circle's catalog, paid over both rails.
- **Testnet**: Circle's catalog has no testnet listings, so Kawase runs its own demo seller (`seller.getkawase.xyz`) to
  try it end to end with test money.
