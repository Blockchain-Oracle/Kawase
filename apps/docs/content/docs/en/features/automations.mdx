Automations are where an agent earns its keep: things that should happen without you opening the app. Every automation
runs **unattended**, but inside an on-chain grant you sign once, so even Kawase itself cannot make it do more.

## Scheduled payments

"Every Friday at 9 send 10 dollars to Alex." Kawase reads it back with the next runs and the maximum it could ever move,
then you turn it on with **one Face ID**. That signs the automation's own **grant**: it may only transfer that token, only
to Alex's address (pinned on chain), and only up to the amount per period. Schedules: every day, every week on a weekday,
or every month on day 1–28, in your own time zone.

Each run happens **exactly once**: if Kawase's server restarts mid-run, the run is recovered under the same id and never
paid twice. Weekly and monthly runs get a **reminder a day before** (with a heads-up when your wallet is short). A run
that can't go ahead is **skipped with a reason** in your inbox, and a run skipped for lack of money is **retried once**
when money arrives.

## Auto-save

"Keep 100 dollars in my wallet and save the rest." Auto-save keeps that amount ready and moves anything above it into your
own vault savings (see [Earn](./earn-and-borrow.mdx)). When your wallet drops below the line, it tops it back up from
savings. It checks daily and whenever money arrives. Its grants only allow moving USDC **between your wallet and your own
savings**, never anywhere else.

## Payroll and DCA

- **Payroll** pays several people on a day of the month. Each payee gets their own grant, pinned to them and their amount,
  and all of them are signed with **one Face ID**. See [Business](./business.mdx#payroll).
- **DCA** ("buy 20 dollars of cirBTC every week") buys a token with a fixed amount on a schedule. Each run waits for you in
  **Needs you** with a fresh executable quote.

## Rules

"When my balance goes above 500, bridge what's above 400 to my Base address." A rule has:

- a **trigger**: the balance goes above or below an amount; money arrives (optionally from someone, or at least an
  amount); money goes out (**round-ups**); or a token's **price** crosses a level (checked on the executable quote every
  two minutes);
- an **action**: bridge USDC to your own address on another network (the route fee is capped on chain), save into your
  vault, **send to someone** (bounded on chain), **swap**, or just alert you;
- an **amount**: what is above a line, a share of the money that arrived, or a fixed amount.

A balance or price rule fires **once per crossing** and re-arms when it goes back. A **round-up** rule ("round up my
payments and save the change") saves the difference from each send into your own vault.

## Practice first

A new automation can start in **practice**: it runs without a grant, and each run is the exact transfer as a dry run on
Arc, recorded as **"would have"**. Nothing moves. When you're happy, **Go live** signs its grant. Ask "what would happen if
this ran now?" for a dry run on demand. With global [practice mode](./limits-and-control.mdx#practice-mode) on, every
automation runs in practice.

## Needs you

An automation never swaps on its own, and never pays above its grant or past your allowlist or new-recipient rules. When a
run reaches such a step it waits in **Needs you** (inbox and Automations, with a push where notifications are set up).
Approving opens the same send or swap you'd make by hand. Unanswered items are reminded after 12 hours and expire after
24. See [Needs you](./limits-and-control.mdx#needs-you).

## Managing them

From the Automations screen or in chat: list, **pause**, **resume**, **skip the next run**, **edit** and **cancel**.

- **Editing** in place: asking for less applies at once; asking for more signs a new grant together with the old one's
  revoke.
- **Cancelling** also **revokes the grant on chain**, so the permission is gone, not just hidden.
