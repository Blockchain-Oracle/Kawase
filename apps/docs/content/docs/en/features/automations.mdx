Automations are things that should happen without you opening the app. Each one runs **unattended**, but inside a
limit your wallet signs once on Arc, so even Kawase itself cannot make it do more.

## How runs behave

- **Exactly once.** If Kawase's server restarts mid-run, the run is recovered under the same id and never paid twice.
- **Reminders.** Weekly and monthly runs get a reminder a day before, with a heads-up when your wallet is short.
- **Skipped with a reason.** A run that can't go ahead is skipped and says why in your inbox; one skipped for lack of
  money is retried once when money arrives.

## Rules and saving

- **Keep and save** (Auto-save): keep an amount ready in your wallet; the rest earns in your own
  [vault savings](./earn-and-borrow.mdx) and comes back when you run low.
- **Round up my payments**: save the change from each payment you send.
- **Save above a line** and **Low balance alert**: act or tell you when your balance crosses a line, once per crossing.
- In chat you can go further: "when my balance goes above 500, bridge what's above 400 to my Base address", or act
  when money arrives or a token's price crosses a level.

## Practice first

A new automation can start in **practice**: each run is the exact transfer as a dry run on Arc, recorded as **"would
have"**. Nothing moves. When you're happy, **turn it on**. Ask "what would happen if this ran now?" any time.

## Needs you

An automation never swaps on its own, and never pays above its grant or past your rules. When a run reaches such a step
it waits in **Needs you** (inbox and Automations, with a push where notifications are on). Approving opens the very
send or swap you'd make by hand, re-checked. Unanswered items are reminded after 12 hours and expire after 24.

## Managing them

From Automations or in chat: list, **pause**, **resume**, **skip the next run**, **edit** and **cancel**. Asking for less
applies at once; asking for more signs a new grant together with the old one's revoke. **Cancelling** also revokes the
grant on chain, so the permission is gone, not just hidden.
