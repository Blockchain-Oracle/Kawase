## Send, step by step

Open **Send** from **+**, or just say it ("send 5 dollars to @ines", "pay Sam 5 euros"). You can send **any token you
hold on Arc**.

### What is checked on every payment

- **Where the address came from.** Kawase only pays an address from your contacts, a handle, a code you scanned, or
  your own message, word for word.
- **The recipient itself.** A token contract or a look-alike of a saved contact is refused; a contract that isn't a
  wallet is warned about; the token issuer's blocklist is checked where it has one; your own address is named as yours.
- **Your money and your rules.** Your balance, your [weekly budget](./limits-and-control.mdx), and whether a contact's
  address changed recently.

## By asking Kawase

Say or type it the way you'd say it to a person. Kawase prepares **the same card**, with the same checks, and waits
for your tap: a spoken "yes" never approves a payment.

Your wallet has **one address on both networks**, and it takes **any token on Arc**.

**Add money** (from **+**) gathers every way in: [buy with Apple Pay or a card](./buy.mdx), from an exchange, USDC
waiting for you on another chain (brought to Arc with no gas), your address and QR code, and test USDC on testnet.
Money that arrives shows up in [Activity](./activity-and-receipts.mdx) and the inbox, can wake your
[automations](./automations.mdx), and offers **Save sender** as a contact.

## Scan to pay

**Scan a QR code** on Send (or in **+**) reads a bare address, a payment link with an amount and token (EIP-681), or a
Kawase request link. A code for the other Arc network, another chain or a closed request says why, with a network
switch where that helps.

## Request and split

"Request 15 dollars from Sam for dinner", or **Request**. Kawase makes a **payment request** in any token on Arc with a
link you share. The person paying opens it in any browser; it is marked paid when a Kawase payment or a matching plain
transfer arrives.

<Shot src="send/request-amount" alt="Request: 15 USDC on the keypad, a note field for the person who pays, and Create request.">

**Split a bill**: "split 30 dollars for dinner with Ines and Sam". Equal shares or by hand, with you included or not.
Each person gets their own request and link, and a progress card shows who has paid.
