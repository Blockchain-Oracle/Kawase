This page is for reviewers who click around without us in the room. Each step says what to do, what you should see, and
where the proof is: the [demo page](https://getkawase.xyz/demo) groups every real Arc testnet transaction behind a claim,
and each step links to its group (for example [`/demo#send`](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#send)). Testnet money has no
value, so nothing here costs anything.

<Callout type="warn" title="Paused on the live app (2026-10-03)">

- **Chat and voice** are paused: the AI model provider's prepaid credit has run out.
- **Anything your wallet signs** is paused: wallet setup, the everyday allowance, and every confirmation (Face ID or the
  email code). Kawase's wallet provider, Turnkey, allows 25 signatures a month on its free plan, and October's are used.
- **Mainnet** moves no money yet: Kawase's mainnet relayer has not been funded.

Until these are lifted, the steps marked **paused** below cannot be run on the live app; their transactions on the demo
page are the proof that they work.

</Callout>

## What you can try right now

Use [app.getkawase.xyz](https://app.getkawase.xyz) in a desktop or phone browser, the
[Android APK](https://expo.dev/accounts/abu.dev/projects/kawase/builds/e644e19d-72ca-4afd-b212-cf35a98b56ed), or
TestFlight if you have an invite.

1. **Arrive as a guest.** Pick a language from the pill on the first screen (Arabic and Urdu turn the whole layout
   right-to-left), swipe the short story, and open every screen: Home, Markets, a token page, Activity, Settings.
2. **Help & trust** (Settings): what Kawase can do, where your money lives, how to leave without Kawase, and the live
   state of Arc, Circle and prices.
3. **Sign in** with an email code (or Apple or Google). Kawase creates your own wallet at Turnkey: one address on both
   networks.
4. **Get test money** from the faucet card on Home: Circle's faucet sends about **20 USDC and 20 EURC**. Your balance,
   the fee chip ("~N actions left": Arc's fees are paid in USDC) and the receive page with your QR code follow.
5. **Switch networks** with the pill in the header: the first switch to mainnet shows the real-money consent.
6. **Check the chain.** Every claim below has a transaction on [/demo](https://getkawase.xyz/demo) that opens on the
   explorer.

## The five-minute path (testnet, paused)

This is the path once chat and wallet signing are back. On the web an **email code** stands in for Face ID; on iPhone and
Android the same steps use Face ID or the fingerprint.

<Steps>
<Step>

### 1. Ask what it can do

> what can you do?

You get the capability card: what works on testnet and on mainnet, from the same registry the agent reads before it
proposes anything ([availability](./availability.mdx)).

Proof: [the demo video](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#video).

</Step>
<Step>

### 2. Set up the wallet and accept the allowance

Kawase upgrades your wallet in place with EIP-7702; you sign once and Kawase's relayer pays the fee. Then you accept the
**everyday allowance** with one confirmation: a delegation that lets Kawase's agent wallet send up to a daily amount of
USDC for you, with the cap, the token, the function, the redeemer and the expiry written on chain.

Proof: [setup paid by the relayer](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#setup), [sends inside the on-chain
allowance](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#allowance).

</Step>
<Step>

### 3. Send money

> send 1 dollar to 0x… (paste an address you control, or a friend's @handle)

Kawase resolves the recipient, runs its checks (balance, limits, look-alike and contract addresses) and shows the card:
amount, currency, recipient, network. Inside the allowance it goes at once and the card follows the transaction to the
explorer; above it, or to a new address above the first-time threshold, it waits for your confirmation. A wallet with no
USDC left for fees still sends USDC or EURC: you sign once and the relayer pays.

Proof: [sends](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#send), including the gas-free one.

</Step>
<Step>

### 4. Swap

> swap 2 dollars to euros

The ticket shows the best quote of KyberSwap, Circle App Kit and LI.FI (the others below it), the minimum you receive and
the fee. Slide to confirm: approve and swap go as **one** EIP-7702 batch, one confirmation. On testnet only USDC ↔ EURC
exists to trade; on mainnet it is any token on Arc, meme coins included, with GoPlus checks and Kawase's copycat rule.

Proof: [swaps](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#swap). Bridges and earn work the same way:
[bridge](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#bridge) ("move 1 dollar to Base"), [earn](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#earn) ("put 5
dollars in savings").

</Step>
<Step>

### 5. Hand it a job

> every day at 9 send 0.05 dollars to the same address

The card shows the schedule, the amount and the **grant** it needs: transfer only, pinned to that one recipient, at most
that amount per period, expiring with the automation. Run it in practice first (a dry run that moves nothing), then turn
it on with one confirmation. It then runs by itself inside the grant; anything it may not do alone waits in **Needs
you**. Also try "keep 20 dollars in my wallet and save the rest" (Auto-save). Cancelling revokes the grant on chain.

Proof: [automations that ran unattended](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#automation), [grants
revoked](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#revoke).

</Step>
<Step>

### 6. Talk to it

Tap the orb in the dock and say, in any of the ten languages, "how much do I have?". It answers aloud and puts the same
cards on screen as chat. Ask it to send money: the card is prepared and **waits for your tap**. A spoken "yes" never
approves a payment.

Proof: [the demo video](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#video).

</Step>
</Steps>

With another minute: **Business** (Settings → Business) makes an invoice with a payment link, a PDF and an Arc Memo
reference that marks it paid when the money lands, and pays several people in one signed batch
([business](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#business)). **Wallet & limits** has Pause all and the exit kit; see
[safety](./safety.mdx).

## The mainnet path

Mainnet is real money, so it takes an account and an explicit consent. Tap the network pill: the first switch shows the
real-money consent; after it, the pill, the REAL MONEY label and the orb's colour all say mainnet. Your address is the
same; balances, history and limits are kept per network ([network toggle](./features/network-toggle.mdx)).

What only mainnet has, each built against mainnet contracts and live quotes:

- **Markets**: every token on Arc with price, chart and safety checks; meme coins; a token still on a launchpad's bonding
  curve shows its progress to graduation ([swap and trade](./features/swap-and-trade.mdx)).
- **Long and short**: Kawase Margin opens a leveraged position through your own Aave V4 account, with the liquidation
  price on the ticket before you slide ([long and short](./features/long-short.mdx)).
- **Earn and borrow**: Aave V4 supply and borrowing with a live health gauge next to Morpho vaults and Aero campaigns
  ([earn and borrow](./features/earn-and-borrow.mdx)).
- **Agent wallet**: paid x402 services from Circle's catalog, inside a daily budget you set
  ([marketplace](./features/marketplace.mdx)).
- **Buy**: USDC with a card through Circle's Onramp, delivered to your own wallet ([buy](./features/buy.mdx)).

Moving real money needs the mainnet wallet setup, which is **paused** with the rest of wallet signing and until the
mainnet relayer is funded; the setup then says "Kawase is getting mainnet ready" instead of failing silently. Every
mainnet action runs the same code as its testnet twin (the network is part of the session, not something the agent
chooses), so the testnet evidence is the proof of the mechanism: [setup](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#setup),
[send](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#send), [swap](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#swap), [earn](https://getkawase.xyz/demo#earn).
No real money has moved through Kawase on mainnet yet.
