Kawase never holds your money. Your tokens sit in **your own account** on Arc, and the permissions you gave the agent are
narrow and expire on their own. This guide takes you out step by step: first with Kawase's help, then without it.

<Status ids="export_wallet" />

## Before you start

Gather what isn't plain money in your wallet yet:

- **Savings** in a vault: withdraw them on the Earn screen.
- **Aave V4** supply or a loan: repay and withdraw on the Borrow screen.
- **Long or short** positions: close them (Close · All) on each position's page.
- **The agent wallet**: Settings → Agent wallet → **Return to my wallet**.
- Any other token can go out with Withdraw as itself on Arc, or converted to USDC to cross to another chain.

## Part 1: with Kawase (the exit kit)

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pause the agent
Settings → **Wallet & limits** → **Pause all**. Everything the agent does without asking stops at once. Your own Face ID
payments still work.
</Step>
<Step>
### Revoke every permission
In the exit kit, **hold to revoke**. One Face ID sends one transaction that revokes every grant on that network: your
allowance and every automation's grant. Automations end. Repeat on the other network if you used it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Withdraw everything
In the exit kit, **move the money out** opens [Withdraw](./features/bridge-and-withdraw.mdx#withdraw) with everything
selected (or say "withdraw everything to my Kraken"). Pick where it goes: an **exchange** (Kraken, Bybit, OKX, Binance,
Bitget, KuCoin, or Coinbase through Base), **your own wallet** on Arc or another chain, or your bank through an exchange.
Kawase plans each token (a transfer, a bridge, or a conversion to USDC first), warns you about anything the destination
wouldn't credit, and you confirm once with Face ID.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the explorer
Open your address on the explorer (below) and confirm the balances are where you sent them.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: without Kawase (export your wallet)

Your wallet's key is held for you by **Turnkey**. Exporting gives you your own copy, so you can use the same account in any
wallet.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Export
Settings → **Account** → **Export wallet**. Read the warnings, confirm with Face ID, and Turnkey decrypts your **recovery
phrase** on your device. Screenshots and screen recording are blocked while it shows, and it hides when you leave. Write
it down somewhere safe offline. Anyone with it controls your money.
</Step>
<Step>
### Import it into another wallet
In MetaMask, Rabby or another wallet that allows custom networks, choose to import a recovery phrase. You get **the same
address** as in Kawase.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add Arc
Add the network with the details in the table below. Your balances appear as they are on chain.
</Step>
<Step>
### Move your money
Send from that wallet as you would from any other. Vault, Aave and margin positions live in your own account at those
protocols, so any interface for them on Arc can manage them.
</Step>
</Steps>

Export while Kawase is running if you want a copy you control: the export goes through the app.

## Check everything on the explorer

You don't need Kawase to see your money. Open your address on Arc's block explorer:

- Mainnet: `https://explorer.arc.io/address/<your address>`
- Testnet: `https://testnet.arcscan.app/address/<your address>`

There you can see:

- your balances (on Arc, USDC is also the network's gas token: one balance, shown both as the native coin and as the USDC
  token);
- every transaction, including the payments the agent made under your allowance;
- your account's **code**: after setup it is `0xef0100` followed by the `EIP7702StatelessDeleGator` address. That is the
  EIP-7702 upgrade; your account still works as a normal account.

Kawase's receipts link to these same pages.

## If Kawase disappears

- **The agent's permissions stop being useful.** Your allowance and automation grants can only be used by Kawase's own agent
  wallet, and they expire: the allowance after 90 days, each automation at its end date.
- **Your money stays in your account.** It is an ordinary account on Arc.
- **With an exported recovery phrase** (part 2), you can move everything from any wallet that supports Arc.

## Adding Arc to another wallet

| | Mainnet | Testnet |
|---|---|---|
| Network name | Arc | Arc Testnet |
| Chain ID | `5042` | `5042002` |
| RPC | `https://rpc.mainnet.arc.io` | `https://rpc.testnet.arc.io` |
| Currency symbol | USDC | USDC |
| Explorer | `https://explorer.arc.io` | `https://testnet.arcscan.app` |

Some wallets label the gas balance "ETH" on custom networks; on Arc it is USDC. The USDC token address is
`0x3600000000000000000000000000000000000000` on both networks; EURC is
`0xbEf5f6d51CB62b58e6A8f77868681825C6fe21c1` on mainnet and `0x89B50855Aa3bE2F677cD6303Cec089B5F319D72a` on testnet.

## Removing the EIP-7702 upgrade (optional)

You don't need to undo the upgrade to move your money. If you want a plain account again after exporting it, sign an
EIP-7702 authorization pointing at the zero address and send it from the account itself, for example with viem:

```ts
import { zeroAddress } from "viem";

const authorization = await walletClient.signAuthorization({ contractAddress: zeroAddress, executor: "self" });
await walletClient.sendTransaction({ to: walletClient.account.address, authorizationList: [authorization] });
```

Afterwards the explorer shows no code on your address.
