# Stage 8: Automations + delegated authority

Purpose: a stage of the Kawase build. Source of truth for scope is `plan/MASTER-PLAN.md`; decisions are in `context/08-product-research/DECISIONS-FROM-ABU.md`.
Status: in progress — 8a scheduled sends and 8b Auto-save (event-triggered, bounded to the person's own savings) done on testnet; price triggers, templates, practice mode and the DBOS executor open.

## Scope (from the master plan)
- `apps/executor` DBOS workflows: tick, run-occurrence, grant-lifecycle.
- `draft_automation` with clarifications (day, time zone, asset), the read-back + dry run + next 3 runs + max exposure, the **delegation grant signed by the user's wallet** (one Face ID) (caveats built by code, receipt decoded from the caveats).
- Scheduled, price-triggered (on the executable quote) and event-triggered (salary received, balance below X) automations; DCA, round-ups, yield sweep, payroll templates (R).
- Lifecycle: pause, resume, edit (narrowing is immediate; widening needs a new grant), skip next, cancel (`disableDelegation`), a 24 h reminder, "skipped because…", underfunded state, missed-run policy.
- Practice mode + the go-live gate. The "Needs you" queue with expiry + re-quote at execution.
- Attended mode (ask every time) for users who prefer it.
- **Exit:** "every Tuesday send $1 to Alex" runs unattended on testnet and mainnet within its caveat; over-cap and wrong-recipient attempts revert on chain; editing never double-pays; practice produces "would have" records.

## Progress log
- **2026-10-01 — 8a scheduled sends, built and verified on Arc testnet.**
  - **The bound is on chain.** Turning an automation on signs `buildAutomationGrant` (already proven by spike 15): a delegation from the person's 7702 wallet to Kawase's ops wallet that may only call `token.transfer(recipient, …)` — the recipient pinned by an `AllowedCalldata` caveat — at most `amount` per period (`ERC20PeriodTransfer`), only redeemable by the ops wallet, until it ends (a year). The period is the schedule's: a day, a week, or 28 days for monthly (a calendar month is never shorter, so two monthly runs never share a period).
  - **Exactly once.** `automation_runs` has a unique (automation, scheduled time) row: the runner claims an occurrence by inserting it, so a second runner, a retry or a re-look finds it taken. Missed occurrences (the runner was down for longer than a period) are recorded as `MISSED_RUN`, not made up. Schedules are computed in the person's own zone with `Intl` only (`packages/intents/schedule.ts`; checked across zones and London's DST change).
  - **Each run re-checks.** `SendService.runAutomation`: the send rules on fresh facts for the automation's own recipient (paused, limits under the per-person lock, a held / blocked / lookalike address, the balance), then the grant's room this period read from the chain (`getAvailableAmount`); a run that cannot go is `skipped` with the reason and an `automation_skipped` inbox item; otherwise the ops wallet redeems the automation's grant through the same attempt → tracking → receipt witness as any send (inbox `send_completed`, contacts marked paid).
  - **Lifecycle:** pause (runs stop at once), resume (next occurrence from now), skip next (recorded as `SKIPPED_BY_YOU`), cancel (runs stop at once; the revoke transaction for the grant is handed to the wallet: `disableDelegation`, one more Face ID; if declined, Kawase still never uses it and says so).
  - **Agent:** `draft_automation` (the address only from the person's own words; 09:00 and their zone when not said — the app now records the device's time zone on the account the first time it sees "UTC"), a read-back summary with the next runs and the bound.

- **2026-10-01 — 8b Auto-save, built and verified on Arc testnet.** "Keep 5 dollars in my wallet and save the rest."
  - **The bound is ownership, enforced on chain.** Three delegations from the wallet to the ops wallet (`packages/integrations/delegation/src/save.ts`), each a `FunctionCall` scope plus timestamp and redeemer caveats: `usdc.approve(vault, …)` with the spender pinned to the vault; `vault.deposit(assets, receiver)` with the receiver pinned to the wallet; `vault.withdraw(assets, receiver, owner)` with both pinned to the wallet. They are separate because a caveat applies to every call a grant allows (one grant could not pin a different argument per method, and would let the vault's own shares be approved away). A move in is two redeems in one `redeemDelegations` call (approve exactly the amount, then deposit it); a move out is one. Even a leaked ops key can only turn the person's USDC into their own vault shares and back. One Face ID signs all three; cancelling revokes all three in one self-batch transaction.
  - **Proof on chain** (`packages/integrations/app-kit/test/auto-save.integration.ts`, 11/11): the ops wallet saved 0.6 USDC into a new wallet's position [`0x2dce…9d43`](https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/0x2dcee603248724bcc4c6b69d5a56cf033500b9e2180b2b06f9824eec6c119d43) and topped it up with 0.25 [`0x6a88…4314`](https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/0x6a88774d1d06e3f35b3f0a627cfb741c0c5c930f4905c2cc4be6e280867c4314); deposit for someone else, approving someone else, withdrawing to someone else, a method the grant does not name, and a redeem by anyone but the ops wallet were all refused by the chain; the wallet revoked all three in one transaction [`0x85c7…c445`](https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/0x85c7e38f5d56feb7c479764cf483317674aa549ffe8e49204b828405b4dec445), after which neither direction worked.

Each check (`AutomationService.runSave`): paused-all stops it; the balance, the position and what can come back (the position capped by App Kit's liquidity — the mock vault's `maxWithdraw` reads 0 while withdrawals work) give the move (`planSave`: above max(keep, gas float) by at least 1 USDC → save the excess; below keep by at least 1 USDC → top up as far as savings allow); nothing to move is recorded quietly. - **2026-10-01 — Review K01: claimed runs are recoverable (J10's expanded review).** A run's operation identity is now fixed at the claim: the run row carries the id its money intent will have, committed in one transaction with the schedule's advance. The send and earn pipelines create the intent under exactly that id (checked under the per-person lock, primary key as the final guard), so doing a run again finds it and resumes — never a second payment; Circle submission was already keyed by the attempt's idempotency key, and the send/earn sweepers resubmit an attempt whose call was lost. The reconciler (replacing `settleRuns`, which inner-joined intents and so never saw an orphan) settles runs from their intent's final state and drives again any claimed run whose intent does not exist after a two-minute lease; a claim from before this change (no id) ends as `RUN_INTERRUPTED` with an inbox item. Auto-save follows a move already made for its run instead of planning again.
