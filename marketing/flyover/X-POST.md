## Which size, where

| Size | Use it for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1440×1152 (5:4)** | **X** | Taller than 16:9, so it fills more of a phone screen in the feed. It sits inside the 2:1 to 3:4 range that X is reported to show uncropped, and it's the size the reference film itself was posted in on X. The film was composed for it; the other two are reframes. |
| 1080×1350 (4:5) | Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads | 4:5 is native in those feeds. On X, sources disagree about whether portrait video gets cropped in the timeline. |
| 1920×1080 (16:9) | YouTube, the landing page, the hackathon page | Landscape players. |

## Why this shape

- **Muted first.** X autoplays videos muted in the timeline and plays sound only when someone taps. The film works
  without sound: frame one already shows the headline "Money that moves when you speak.", and the camera and the logo
  tiles start moving within a tenth of a second. Whip is the reward for people who tap.
- **The first line says what it is.** A first post can't count on anyone knowing the name. "Introducing Kawase" plus
  the category line answers "what is this?" before the video's first second ends.
- **Three sentences people would actually say.** These are the landing's own examples. A reader can picture saying one
  of them, and that does more than a list of features.
- **The trust line last.** "Inside limits you sign" answers the first worry anyone has about an AI touching their money.
- **The link goes in the reply.** That's the campaign's rule, so the post itself stays a clean video post.

Every claim comes from the landing's own copy (tour step 1, the hero, "You see each answer by name"). The post makes no
iPhone or mainnet promises.
